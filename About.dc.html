Get in touch to bring your vision to life.

Tesseract is an integrated real estate development firm that designs and builds fire resilient schools, homes and communities that are beautiful, luxurious, and sustainable.

We integrate leading-edge building design, energy and water harvesting, permaculture, and circular flows of material resources.

We utilize procedural architecture and artificial intelligence to optimize spatial layouts and material flows on site.

We are a team of experts who have designed, developed, served as general contractor, and served as architect and landscape architect for diverse projects, including retreat centers, apartment buildings, corporate campuses, single family homes, and ADUs.

We believe that we can affordably build better communities that dramatically elevate quality of life, productivity, and ecological balance.
